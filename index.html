    Common Questions

    Is my data safe?

    Instant scoring runs in your browser. AI review sends only the text you paste to Claude, and we never sell or share your information.

    Do I need to connect LinkedIn?

    No. You paste the text you want reviewed, so there's no login or permissions to grant.

    How is the score calculated?

    Headline (20%), About (30%), Skills (20%) and Impact in your experience (30%) are checked for length, clarity, keywords and measurable results. AI review then adds specific feedback and a rewritten headline.

    Can I re-run the audit?

    Absolutely. Edit your text and analyze again to see how each change moves your score.
